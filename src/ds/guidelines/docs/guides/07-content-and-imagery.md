# Content & imagery

## Photography

- **Dignity first.** Show children as children — playing, laughing, hugging, creating — not as patients. No needles, no suffering, no "before" shots. A hospital setting is fine when the moment in it is warm.
- Eye-level, natural light, real moments from the amuta's activities beat stock. If stock or generated imagery is unavoidable, it must not imply a real identified child.
- **Consent**: every identifiable child needs written parental consent; when in doubt use hands, backs, activities, volunteers.
- Warm colour grade; let the logo colours appear in props (balloons, shirts, crafts) rather than in filters.
- Crops: hero photo 4:5 in the petal mask — keep faces in the upper-centre (`position="center top"`); cards 3:2; gallery 4:3 or 1:1.
- No text baked into images. No photo without `alt`.
- Until a real photo exists, leave `src` empty — `Picture` renders the branded butterfly placeholder, which is always better than a grey box or an off-brand stock image.

## Writing for donors

- Make amounts tangible: tie each preset in `DonationForm.impact` to a real outcome.
- Trust signals near every donation action: "תשלום מאובטח", "סעיף 46", registration number, links to `DocumentCard`s.
- Default to monthly giving, default amount ₪180; never pre-select the highest amount.
- After donating: `Dialog` thank-you with the receipt line and a share action.

## Writing for families

- The first screen of any help page answers: what do we offer, to whom, how to reach us — with a phone number and WhatsApp visible (`FloatingContact`).
- Avoid medical jargon; explain processes with `Steps`.
- FAQ answers (`Accordion`) are 2–4 sentences, first sentence is the answer.

## Numbers

Use real, current numbers and name the year. Round honestly ("1,250+", not "1,253"). Four stats maximum in a row.
